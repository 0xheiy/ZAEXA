QUESTIONS

The ones worth asking.

Do you hold my money?⌄

No. Your tokens leave your wallet, move through the pools, and come back to your wallet inside a single transaction. Nothing of yours sits in the contract between transactions. There is no account, no deposit, and no backend — the app runs entirely in your browser.

What is the Exit check?⌄

Before you buy, it buys the token and sells it straight back inside one simulated transaction, then shows what that round trip actually costs. A token that cannot be sold back shows up right there. It is the honeypot test most interfaces do not run.

What can the owner do?⌄

Nothing to your funds. The contract holds nothing between transactions, and the owner cannot take tokens out of your wallet, cannot pause you, and cannot change the code — the contract is not upgradeable.

Has the contract been audited?⌄

No. The source is public and verified on BaseScan, and it is covered by tests — but no third party has audited it. That is a real difference, and you should size your trades accordingly.

What are the fees?⌄

You pay the pools' own fees, which are shown next to each venue, and network gas.

What do you collect about me?⌄

Anonymous counts, and only a few things: which view was opened, a few funnel steps, a four-character code identifying which JS bug fired, whether the screen is mobile or desktop, and a country code. Never your wallet address, the amount, the token, or your IP.
